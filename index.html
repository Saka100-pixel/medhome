How it works

From request to bedside in four steps.

01

Search for care

Choose the type of professional you need, your area in Lusaka, and when you need care. See verified professionals available near you instantly.

02

Every profile shows the real license number, NMCZ or HPCZ verification badge, qualifications, experience, and reviews from past families.
