How to join

Start earning in four steps.

01

Submit your application

Fill in your name, discipline, and contact details. Upload your NMCZ or HPCZ certificate and your NRC or passport.

02

We verify your license

MedHome checks your license directly against the NMCZ or HPCZ database. Usually 24 to 48 hours. Once verified, your profile goes live with a badge.

03

Set up your profile

Add your photo, set your hourly rate, choose the areas of Lusaka you cover, and mark your availability. You are now visible to families searching for care.

04

Accept and get paid

When a family books you, accept the shift, deliver the care, and your payment arrives in your mobile money account automatically when the shift is done.

Common questions

Everything you need to know.

How long does verification take?

Usually 24 to 48 hours. We check directly with NMCZ or HPCZ. Once verified your profile goes live with a verification badge immediately.

Do I need my own transport?

Yes. You are responsible for getting to and from the patient's home. You can factor travel costs into your rate when setting your price.

When do I get paid?

Payment is released to your MTN or Airtel mobile money account automatically 2 hours after the shift is completed, unless a dispute is raised.

Can I choose my own working hours?

Completely. You set your availability day by day. You accept only the bookings that suit you. There is no minimum number of shifts required.

What happens if a family cancels?

If a family cancels with less than 4 hours notice, they pay a cancellation fee which is passed to you. Last-minute cancellations will not cost you your time.

Can I work on MedHome alongside my hospital job?

Yes. MedHome is designed for exactly this. Many professionals take shifts on weekends, evenings, or days off. You are fully in control of when you work.
