How to join

Start earning in four steps.

01

Submit your application

Fill in your name, discipline, and contact details. Upload your NMCZ or HPCZ certificate and your NRC or passport.

02

We verify your license

MedHome checks your license directly against the NMCZ or HPCZ database. Usually 24 to 48 hours. Once verified, your profile goes live with a badge.

03

Set up your profile

Add your photo, set your hourly rate, choose the areas of Lusaka you cover, and mark your availability. You are now visible to families searching for care.

04

Accept and get paid

When a family books you, accept the shift, deliver the care, and your payment arrives in your mobile money account automatically when the shift is done.
